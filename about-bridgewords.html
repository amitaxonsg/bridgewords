About BridgeWords

Reputation takes years to build and seconds to damage.

BridgeWords helps individuals and organisations manage risks, mitigate issues and handle crises through disciplined, business-focused communication.

“Lose money for the firm, and I will be understanding. Lose a shred of reputation for the firm, and I will be ruthless.” — Warren Buffett

Building a brand is a long and strategic process, but a hard-earned reputation can evaporate quickly if a company mishandles a crisis or ignores an emerging issue.

An indiscreet moment caught on camera, a serious operational failure or a product safety issue can place individuals and companies under immediate scrutiny. The communications response must be as disciplined as the operational response.

At BridgeWords, our mission is to help clients manage risks, mitigate issues and handle crises. We are communication experts who focus on business outcomes. No spin, no fluff: only strategies that deliver results.

Your needs determine how we work with you. We can be your fractional head of communications, your strategic adviser or your ad hoc communications consultant.

Investing in expert communication advice—especially when dark clouds appear—is money well spent. The alternative can be immediate financial loss and longer-term brand erosion.
